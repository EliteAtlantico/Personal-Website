Going from Kuwait to Toronto was a huge shift; I went from having to spread awareness and convince people of why they should care about technology, through my work making hackathons and starting a robotics club and helping them get into competitions, to an environment where people already love this stuff and are ready to give their all for this. I loved this change from the very beginning, and I took (and am still taking) advantage of this new sentiment every chance I get. Being able to surround myself with people who care about their work as much as I do has been thrilling, not to mention the newfound competition that is forcing me to climb to heights I never thought I could reach. That's not to say I don't miss Kuwait and the Middle East as a whole; I loved every second of my time there, and having to preach about technology to them only deepened my love for the field and has likely played a big role in how passionate I am about it. Though I'll always miss the feeling of the Kuwait sun on my skin, I wouldn't trade the freezing temperatures of Toronto for anything.
