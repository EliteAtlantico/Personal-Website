## The desktop
Arch Linux on Hyprland (83 keybinds and counting), with Waybar, Ghostty, Starship and JetBrains Mono. The same machine runs local LLMs with vLLM, SGLang and llama.cpp, and it's about to host this website.

## The phone
Stock Android wasn't mine enough, so I went further than a launcher. I cross-compiled static ARM64 builds of curl and wget with the Android NDK, and wrote a tiny rootless package installer (jq, fd, ripgrep, bat…) that runs through a patched Shizuku shell.
